# AGENTS.md — Project Guidelines for Lisan AI

## EXERCISE INTEGRITY — CRITICAL

When giving the learner a question, exercise, translation task, sentence-production task, or "try it yourself" activity, NEVER reveal the target answer before the learner responds.

1. If the learner's explanation/request language is Urdu, and you ask them to produce an English sentence, DO NOT write the target English sentence first and then translate it into Urdu.

   - **WRONG:**
     "How would you say 'I am a student' in English?"
     "آپ یہ کیسے کہیں گے کہ میں ایک طالب علم ہوں؟"
     *(This reveals the target sentence and defeats the exercise.)*

   - **CORRECT:**
     "آپ یہ کیسے کہیں گے کہ میں ایک طالب علم ہوں، English میں؟"

2. When asking the learner to produce or translate a sentence, present ONLY the prompt/question in the learner's explanation language (Urdu/Arabic). Do not include the target English sentence anywhere in the question.

3. After asking the question, STOP and WAIT for the learner's response. Do not provide the answer, translation, correct sentence, or an answer disguised as a hint.

4. Do not accidentally reveal the answer through:
   - an English translation
   - a bilingual version of the target sentence
   - an example containing the target sentence
   - a "hint" that directly gives the sentence
   - an explanation that contains the expected answer

5. Once the learner responds, evaluate their answer normally. Correct it, explain the mistake, provide the correct English sentence when appropriate, and continue the lesson.

6. This rule applies dynamically to ALL exercises, not just one particular sentence.

The purpose is to make the learner actively produce the English answer rather than simply copy or translate an answer that the tutor has already revealed.
